# naija-life-sim

**Naija Life — Hustle & Survive** is a browser-based life simulation game built in pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 📋 How to Play

1. **Open `index.html`** in your web browser.
2. **Choose actions** each turn to survive and earn money.
3. **Balance your stats**:
   - **Cash**: Earn money to reach ₦3,000
   - **Hunger**: Keep it high or you'll weaken
   - **Energy**: Rest or you'll crash
   - **Mood**: Do things you enjoy
   - **Health**: Exercise and eat well
   - **Reputation**: Study and network to unlock better opportunities

4. **Win Condition**: Reach ₦3,000 without collapsing.
5. **Lose Condition**: Let your health or hunger drop to 0.

## 🎮 Available Actions

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 💼 **Work** | Steady income (+₦200), drains energy |
| 🚀 **Hustle** | Risky but rewarding. Can earn ₦350 or lose money |
| 🍜 **Eat** | Costs ₦150 but restores hunger and boosts mood |
| 😴 **Rest** | Restores energy and health. Necessary for survival |
| 📚 **Study** | Builds reputation. Unlocks better opportunities |
| 💪 **Exercise** | Improves health and mood |
| 🏪 **Market** | Buy and resell goods for quick profit |
| 👥 **Network** | Build reputation and connections |
| 🎲 **Gamble** | High risk. Can win ₦500 or lose big. Don't make it a habit |

## 💡 Tips to Win

1. **Don't skip meals** — Hunger kills faster than anything else.
2. **Sleep matters** — Rest regularly to maintain energy.
3. **Avoid gambling** — It feels good when you win but destroys your funds when you lose.
4. **Balance work and hustle** — Work is steady; hustles are risky but more rewarding.
5. **Build reputation** — Study and network to unlock better money-making opportunities.
6. **Use the market** — Reselling can be profitable if you're lucky.
